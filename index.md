WoW Forever hands you a lot of consumables, camp objects and class tools, and they eat action bar space fast. Each kit gathers one set of them behind a single button: click it to fan the kit out, click again to pack it away. Kits pick your best item for each slot, warn you when a buff is missing, and stay out of the way in combat.
