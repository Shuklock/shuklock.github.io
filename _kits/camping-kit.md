  - type: text
    heading: Setting it up
    body: |-
      Assign items by dragging them in or right-clicking a slot. You can keep slots visible while the tent is closed, move and resize it, pick square or round slots, and turn timers, glows and profession buttons on or off. Every slot can have a keybind.

      The kit hides when combat starts and comes back when it ends. `/ck` opens settings and `/ck help` lists the rest.
